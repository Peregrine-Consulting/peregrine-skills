# Objections on a first call

Load when writing the objections section of a brief. Pick the three most likely for this lead, and adapt the answer to what you know about them. Answers are short on purpose: acknowledge, one sentence of substance, a question that keeps the conversation going. Never argue.

| They say | What it usually means | Answer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "We're not interested." | You are a stranger and they are busy. | "Fair enough. Can I ask, is it that calls and leads are handled well already, or just not a priority right now?" |
| "Send me some information." | Polite exit, or genuine but busy. | "Happy to. So I send the right thing, what would make this worth 15 minutes to you?" Then send one page, not a deck, and book the follow-up. |
| "We already have someone for that." | They have a receptionist, an agency or a tool. | "Makes sense. Most of the practices we talk to do. The gap is usually nights, lunch and the overflow when two lines ring at once. How are those handled today?" |
| "AI sounds robotic. Our customers won't like it." | Fear of looking cheap. | "That was true a year or two ago. Easiest way to judge is to hear it. Want me to have it call you right now?" Only if a demo line exists and they agree to the call. |
| "How much is it?" | Interest, or a way to disqualify you. | Give the honest range if offer.md has one, then: "It depends on call volume and locations. The 15-minute look tells us both whether it's worth it." Never invent a price. |
| "We're too small / too busy right now." | Timing. | "Understood. When things are busy is usually when calls get missed. Would it make sense to look at it in a few weeks?" Get a date. |
| "Is this a sales call?" | Testing honesty. | "It is. I'll be quick, and if it's not relevant I'll leave you alone." |
| "Who gave you my number?" | Privacy concern. | "Your number is on your Google listing. I called the business line. Happy to take you off my list if you prefer." And do it. |
| "Our patients' data can't leave our system." (healthcare) | HIPAA concern, a real one. | "Right. Anything touching patient information runs under a signed BAA, and the receptionist only collects what your front desk would. We can walk through exactly what it sees." Only say this if it is true for the user's offer. |

Two rules for every answer:
- Never claim a feature, result, certification or integration the user's offer does not have. If unsure, say "let me check and come back to you" and add it to the brief's Unknowns.
- Any "stop contacting me" is final on every channel. Mark the lead do-not-contact.
