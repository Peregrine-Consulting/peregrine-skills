# Business file templates

Load before writing ./business/. Keep headings; drop sections that truly do not apply. Every fact ends with a source in parentheses. Use "GAP:" for anything you could not find.

    (Booking software, CRM, chat widget, call tracking numbers, pixel. Check page source for scripts.)
