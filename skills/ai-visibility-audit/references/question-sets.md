# Question set patterns

Load this in Phase 1 when building the frozen question set.

## The shapes that pull named recommendations

Questions that ask for a provider get named businesses back. Pricing and "how does X work" questions mostly get generic tables with no names, which makes empty, weak cells in the report. Keep at least two thirds of the set provider-seeking.

1. Best / top: "Who is the best [service] in [city]?" "Top-rated [service] near [neighborhood]"
2. Situation: "I need a [service] who can [specific job] in [city]. Who should I call?"
3. Comparison: "[Business] vs [Competitor] for [job]" (only when the brand already has some awareness)
4. Trust: "Which [service] in [city] has the best reviews for [job]?"
5. Constraint: "[service] in [city] that takes [insurance plan / financing / weekends / Spanish]"
6. Brand check (one only): "What do people say about [Business] in [city]?" This one tells you what the engines know about the entity, not whether they recommend it. Keep it out of the visibility rate; report it separately.

## Starters by vertical (rewrite in the buyer's words from ./business/customers.md)

Dental: best family dentist in [area]; emergency dentist open Saturday in [city]; dentist that takes [plan] in [city]; best place for Invisalign in [city].

Medical / specialty clinic: best [specialty] in [city]; [procedure] doctor in [city] with good reviews; who should I see for [symptom] in [city]; vein clinic that takes Medicare in [city].

Surgery center / outpatient: best outpatient surgery center for [procedure] in [city]; where can I get [procedure] done outside a hospital in [city].

Law: best [practice area] lawyer in [city]; who should I hire for [situation] in [state]; top-rated [practice] attorney near [area].

Home services: best [trade] in [city]; emergency [trade] near [area] open now; who installs [product] in [city]; [trade] in [city] with financing.

Med spa / wellness: best place for [treatment] in [city]; [treatment] near me with real before and after photos.

B2B / agency: best [service] agency for [industry]; who can [job] for a [size] company.

## Rules for freezing the set

- 10 to 15 questions. Fewer is noise, more is cost without new information.
- Freeze them verbatim in the audit file. A re-run months later asks exactly these, so change is measurable.
- One topic beats a spread. Twelve questions on the service the business most wants to sell give a clearer fix list than twelve across everything it does.
- Write them the way a person talks to an assistant: full sentences, real situations. "emergency dentist phoenix" and "My tooth cracked on a Sunday, which dentist in north Phoenix can see me today?" retrieve different sources. Keep both forms when the buyer uses both.
- For a local business, include the city or neighborhood in the question and also set the location on the engine where possible. Report which you did.
