# Niche scorecard: anchors and a worked example

Load when scoring candidate niches. All numbers here are illustrative anchors, not market data. Replace them with what you find for the region.

## Anchors per factor

**Value of one customer to them (weight 3)**
- 1: under $200 per customer, rarely repeat (a single haircut, a takeout order)
- 3: $1,000 to $5,000 per job or patient year (HVAC repair, general dental)
- 5: $10,000+ per case or contract (surgery, personal injury, solar, roofing replacement, implants)

**Cost of the problem (weight 3)**
- 1: an annoyance, no clear dollar cost
- 3: one part-time role or a handful of lost jobs a month
- 5: multiple full-time staff on the task, or lost cases worth tens of thousands a year, or unpaid claims sitting in receivables

**Ability to pay (weight 2)**
- 1: owner-operator, thin margins
- 3: one location, 5 to 15 staff
- 5: several locations or providers, an office manager or ops lead, existing software spend

**Reachability (weight 2)**
- 1: no public list, decision maker hidden
- 3: listed on Google Maps, generic emails only
- 5: complete directories, owners named on sites, active on LinkedIn, answer the phone

**Your unfair advantage (weight 2)**
- 1: none
- 3: you understand the work or know a few people in it
- 5: you have a result, a warm intro path, or worked inside one

**Speed of proof (weight 1)**
- 1: results take 6+ months to show
- 5: visible in weeks (calls answered, leads contacted, bookings)

**Compliance drag (weight 1)**
- 1: heavy (patient data, financial data, strict advertising rules) and you have no compliant stack yet
- 5: light

Max score: 5 x (3+3+2+2+2+1+1) = 70.

## Worked example (illustrative)

Candidate A: single-location coffee shops. Value 1, cost 1, pay 1, reach 5, advantage 2, speed 3, compliance 5. Score 3+3+2+10+4+3+5 = 30.

Candidate B: multi-provider specialty clinics in one state. Value 5, cost 5, pay 5, reach 4, advantage 2, speed 4, compliance 2. Score 15+15+10+8+4+4+2 = 58.

Candidate C: residential roofing companies. Value 5, cost 4, pay 4, reach 5, advantage 1, speed 4, compliance 5. Score 15+12+8+10+2+4+5 = 56.

B and C are close. Pick B if you can set up a compliant stack (BAA with your voice and messaging vendors) and have any way in. Pick C if you know a roofer. Do not pick A.

## Questions that break ties
- Who in this niche could introduce me to three others?
- Can I find 500 of them in my target region today?
- What is the one number the owner checks every week, and can I move it?
