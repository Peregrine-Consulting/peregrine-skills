# Signals: how to tell a business is losing customers

Load this to explain a signal, tune weights for an offer, or add niche-specific signals.

The best cold lead is not the biggest business. It is a healthy business that is leaking customers it already paid to attract, where your offer closes the leak. Signals are evidence of the leak. Each one must be true and checkable, because the lead-brief and cold-email skills will quote it to the owner.

## Built into the script

| Signal | What it usually means | Offers it points to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| rating under 4.2 on 20+ reviews | real reputation problem, buyers pick a competitor before calling | reviews and reputation, AI visibility |
| fewer than 15 Google reviews | new or invisible, loses the comparison on Maps and in AI answers | review generation, AI visibility |
| 5+ one and two star reviews | read them: phone and wait complaints are the strongest sales signal you will find | voice receptionist, speed to lead |
| Google profile not claimed | nobody is minding the front door | local SEO, AI visibility |
| no website / site down | every ad and referral lands nowhere | website, AI visibility |
| no online booking on homepage | after-hours buyers cannot book, they call someone else | AI receptionist, booking flows |
| pays for ads, no call tracking | spending money with no idea what it returns | ads management, call tracking, speed to lead |
| site not mobile ready | most local searches are on a phone | website |
| closed weekends | Saturday calls go to voicemail; urgent buyers do not leave messages | AI receptionist |

## Worth checking by hand on A-grade leads

- **Review text.** Search the reviews for "couldn't get through", "never called back", "voicemail", "on hold", "rude", "wait". Three or more mentions in the last year is the single best opener for a voice or speed-to-lead offer. Quote the theme, never a reviewer's name.
- **Ads running.** Meta Ad Library (search the business name) and a Google search for their main service in their city. Active ads plus a weak landing page or no call tracking is the lead-flow angle.
- **Hiring.** A job post for a receptionist, front desk, intake coordinator or call center rep means they are paying to solve the exact problem an AI receptionist solves.
- **AI visibility.** Ask ChatGPT, Gemini or Perplexity "best <niche> in <city>". If competitors are named and they are not, that is a concrete, screenshot-able opener.
- **Growth.** A new location, a new provider or attorney, an expansion announcement. Growth breaks front offices.

## Tuning for the offer

Default weight is 10 per signal, capped at 30. Change it when the offer clearly cares more about one signal. Say so in one line. Examples:

- Voice receptionist or answering: phone complaints in reviews 20, closed weekends 15, no booking 10.
- AI visibility or SEO: under 15 reviews 15, no website 15, unclaimed 10.
- Ads: pays for ads without call tracking 20, rating under 4.2 is a warning (ads to a bad reputation burn money), not a plus.

Never treat having tools as a negative. A business that already pays for booking software, a CRM or ads has budget and has bought software before. That is a buying signal, and the angle becomes consolidation or better results, not "you need your first system".
